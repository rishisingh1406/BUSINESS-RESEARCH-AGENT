# Problem Statement

## 1. User

Individual investors who want to analyze publicly listed companies.

## 2. User Goal

Get enough factual and understandable information to make a more informed investment decision.

The system should help evaluate whether the company is fundamentally attractive, risky, or worth further research.

## 3. Current Process

Find company
↓
Find reports / filings
↓
Read financials
↓
Research management
↓
Research industry & competitors
↓
Calculate metrics
↓
Estimate valuation
↓
Form thesis
↓
Cross-check evidence


## 4. Problems

* Research is time-consuming.
* Information is fragmented across many sources.
* Financial and business information can be difficult to interpret.
* Evidence behind claims is difficult to trace.
* Important information can be missed or misunderstood.

## 5. Core Problem

Investors need to manually collect, interpret, compare, and validate large amounts of company information before forming an investment view.

## 6. Consequences

Poor research can lead to:

* Incomplete understanding
* Missed risks
* Unsupported conclusions
* Inconsistent analysis

## 7. Desired Outcome

Generate a comprehensive, structured, evidence-backed company research report comparable to a high-quality paid research report.

## 8. System Boundary

**In scope:** company research, financial analysis, business analysis, industry/competitor research, valuation, evidence, thesis and antithesis.

**Out of scope:** portfolio allocation, personal financial planning, autonomous trade execution, and determining how much the user should invest.
